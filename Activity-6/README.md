# Activity 6 — BP AWARE: Know Your Numbers. Protect Your Health.

**Course:** GE 4120 — The 21st Century IT Skills

## About this activity
BP AWARE is a blood pressure awareness website made to help people understand blood pressure readings, proper measurement, risk factors, prevention, and management of hypertension. It encourages healthy habits and reminds people when to seek medical attention.

## Website content
The submitted project shows information about hypertension and a website created with Replit, including screenshots of the pages and development work.

## What have I learned?
Creating the BP AWARE website helped me understand how technology can be used to share health information. As a fourth-year nursing student, I learned how to present topics such as blood pressure monitoring, risk factors, and prevention in a way that people can understand. One challenge in making a website is arranging the information so it is both useful and interesting. I can improve by reviewing the layout and making the information easier to navigate. I can use these skills to educate patients and communities in the future.
